Wheel Building

Wheels built your way

The shop side of Singlespeeed: mountain bike wheels, hand-built around your riding. Tell me your bike, your terrain, your weight — I'll spec and build a sets that lasts.

What I build & offer

New wheelsets

Singlespeed MTB wheelsets

Built from hub, rim, and spoke choices that match your frame (boost & non-boost) and riding style.

Custom build

Hub & rim combos

Pick your hubs and rims, or let me spec from what I've ridden and trust. Honest recommendations either way.

Service

Truing, tension & rebuilds

Loose spokes, out-of-true wheels, damaged rims — bring them in for service or a full rebuild.

How ordering works

Step 1

Tell me the details

Frame, fork, brake type, hubs/rims you want (or let me choose), weight, riding style.

Step 2

I quote it

A clear quote — parts + build time, and options if you want to swap anything.

Step 3

Build & deliver

Built, tensioned, and trued to spec. Pick up locally or arrange shipping.

Wheel info I'll need

  • Rider weight + intended terrain (trail, all-mountain, enduro, gravel…)
  • Frame and fork specs (axle type/boost, brake mount — disc mounts help)
  • Hub or rim preferences, if you have them
  • Budget range — I'll work to it honestly
